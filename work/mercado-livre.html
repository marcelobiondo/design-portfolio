Returns

Returns

01

Building a new returns operation from the ground up.

The challenge combined a significant business opportunity with an operational process that was fragmented, partially offline and vulnerable to errors and fraud.

The goal was to design both the ideal operational process and the system supporting it, while creating a model that could scale consistently across six countries.

The challenge

A business problem disguised as an operations problem.

The existing returns process lacked traceability and control, creating inefficiencies and financial risk.

At the same time, there was an estimated multi-million-dollar opportunity tied to bringing returns, triage and product destination processes closer to the operation.

Leadership

I led two senior designers while acting as one of the central leaders of the multidisciplinary group.

My work moved between hands-on execution, mentoring and facilitating decisions across Product, Operations, Operational Excellence and Technology.
